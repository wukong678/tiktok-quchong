# TikTok Video Dedup · Short Drama Dedup · Facebook Reels Dedup

A SEO landing page for creators and overseas short-drama teams who need to **deduplicate TikTok videos, short dramas, and Facebook Reels** without getting flagged by platform AI.

## Why This Repo Exists

Platforms like **TikTok** and **Facebook** use AI-based visual + audio similarity detection to flag re-uploaded or reposted content and throttle its reach. This repo aggregates practical tooling and strategies for:

- **TikTok video dedup** — change the visual & audio fingerprint so TikTok's AI cannot match your clip.
- **Short drama dedup** — process full short-drama episodes while keeping the storyline intact.
- **Facebook short drama dedup / Facebook Reels dedup** — avoid Facebook's repeated-content penalty.
- **Overseas short drama repost** — batch translate, dub, and repurpose Chinese short dramas.

## Dedup Strategy (Three Fingerprint Layers)

1. **Visual fingerprint** — edge obfuscation, mask offset, local warp, picture-in-picture, filter perturbation.
2. **Audio fingerprint** — vocal separation, BGM replacement, tempo shift.
3. **Temporal fingerprint** — frame抽 / 插帧, tempo change, segment reorder, keyframe reshuffle.
